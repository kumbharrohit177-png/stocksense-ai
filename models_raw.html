How the Models Differ in Financial Modeling

Market data exhibits high non-stationarity, kurtosis, and sudden regime transitions. Here is how each algorithmic framework handles financial dynamics.

linear_scale

Linear Regression

Provides extreme inference speed (sub-millisecond) and pristine interpretability of individual beta weights.

Core Limitation: Assumes homoscedasticity and strictly linear relationships. Severely underperforms during black swan volatility spikes, fat-tail drawdowns, and structural market regime switches.
Target Fit: High-frequency baseline arbitrage
auto_graph

ARIMA Models

Gold standard for classical econometric mean-reversion analysis. Highly effective at isolating pure time-series autocorrelation and seasonality through autoregressive integrated operators.

Core Limitation: Requires strict stationarity transformations (differencing). Highly sensitive to hyperparameter choices (p, d, q) and fails to ingest multi-variate external market indicators simultaneously.
Target Fit: Mean-reverting statistical pairs & stationary indices
psychology

Deep Recurrent LSTM

Employs input, forget, and output gating mechanisms that retain long-range temporal context across 60+ sequential days. Accurately maps subtle momentum build-ups, support test exhaustion, and non-linear feature interactions.

Core Strength: Excels at modeling complex non-linear sequence dependencies and multi-variate feature integration (volume, order depth, VWAP).
Target Fit: Volatility regime forecasting & trend continuation
